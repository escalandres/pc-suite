Features

Local Share

Send files between machines with PCSuite on your LAN: no cloud, mutual TLS, a Bluetooth-style pairing code and SHA-256 integrity.

Antivirus

Built-in ClamAV: quick and full scans with live progress, quarantine and definition updates.

Software

Search, install, update and uninstall Windows apps with WinGet, with on-demand UAC elevation.

WSL

Subsystem status, distribution listing and updates — all without leaving the app.

SSH

Generate keys, inspect fingerprints and import public keys into your ~/.ssh.

PGP

Encryption, signing and verification 100% in-process (BouncyCastle). Your keyring never leaves your profile.

Cleanup

Safe sweep of system temporaries, honoring ACLs: whatever it cannot delete, it reports.

Security

Defender, firewall, antivirus and ports — a read-only panel to know your posture.

Plugins

Extend PCSuite with your own modules: detection, update and launch integrated.

Tools

Utilities with parameter schemas: the UI generates the form, the typed bridge executes.

How to update

From the app

On launch, PCSuite checks for news (one minimal GET, nothing more) and gives you a discreet notice. Downloads with built-in SHA-256 verification and installs only when you confirm.

Manual (always available)

Download the installer above and run it: it detects the previous version and replaces it cleanly. Settings, paired devices and history live in your profile and are left untouched.

Changelog

Every release archives its manifest and notes: release notes … · full version history.

Requires Windows 10/11 x64 · WebView2 Runtime (preinstalled with Edge)

No telemetry: PCSuite sends nothing to any server except the update check (and only with your consent to install).
